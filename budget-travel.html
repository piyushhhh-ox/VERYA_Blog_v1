10 Budget-Friendly Destinations in India

A starting point for choosing your next destination without letting the budget decide everything.

Budget travel is less about choosing the cheapest possible option and more about matching the destination, duration and travel style to the amount you want to spend.

Start with the big three

Estimate transport, accommodation and food first. Then keep a smaller portion for activities and unexpected expenses.

Choose the trip before the destination

A two-day nearby escape can feel very different from a five-day trip that needs expensive transport. VERYА uses this kind of information when suggesting destinations.

DESTINATION GUIDE · 5 MIN READ

3 Days in Jaipur: A First-Timer's Guide

A simple structure for exploring Jaipur without trying to see everything at once.

Day 1 — Old Jaipur

Start around the historic centre and build your day around Hawa Mahal, City Palace and nearby markets.

Day 2 — Forts and viewpoints

Give the second day to Amber Fort and the surrounding sights, leaving time for a slower evening.

Day 3 — Food, culture and a flexible finish

Use your last day for local food, photography and any places you missed.

BUDGET TRAVEL · 6 MIN READ

How to Plan a Trip Under ₹15,000

A simple framework for turning one total number into a realistic trip budget.

1. Set the transport ceiling

Decide how much of the total budget can go toward getting there and back before choosing the destination.

2. Protect the stay budget

Compare the number of nights with the type of stay you actually need. A shorter trip can sometimes free more money for experiences.

3. Leave room for the trip itself

Food, local travel and activities should have their own allocation so your entire budget is not spent before the trip begins.
